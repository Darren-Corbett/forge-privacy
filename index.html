Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Doughnut Forge is built on a zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. This means we do not collect, transmit, store, or have any access to your data.

1. Data Storage & Deletion

All cryptographic keys, .vltc2 identities, and .vlt2 files generated by Doughnut Forge are stored exclusively on your local machine. No information ever leaves your device unless you explicitly export and share a file yourself. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no user data is retained on any servers. You can completely delete your local identities and files by uninstalling the application from your Mac.

2. Analytics and Telemetry

Doughnut Forge contains no tracking scripts, analytics engines, or crash reporters. We do not monitor your usage or behavior.

3. Support & Contact

If you have questions or wish to report an issue, please open a ticket directly on our GitHub Issues tracker. You can also contact Darren Corbett directly at darrentcorbett@gmail.com.
