Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Forge is built on a zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. This means we do not collect, transmit, store, or have any access to your data.

1. Data Storage

All cryptographic keys, .vltc2 identities, and .vlt2 files generated by Forge are stored exclusively on your local machine. No information ever leaves your device unless you explicitly export and share a file yourself.

2. Analytics and Telemetry

Forge contains no tracking scripts, analytics engines, or crash reporters. We do not monitor your usage or behavior.

3. Support & Contact

If you have questions or wish to report an issue, please open a ticket directly on our GitHub Issues tracker.
